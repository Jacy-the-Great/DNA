## What Land iQ Is

Land iQ is a NSW Government-built SaaS planning and property analytics platform. Gives planners and built environment professionals access to 200+ authoritative government data sources, interactive 3D mapping, site search, scenario planning, spatial dashboards, and property/portfolio analysis in a single licence.

Built in partnership with WSP (development), Giraffe Technologies (spatial viewer platform), and Aerometrex (3D/aerial). Won the PIA National Award for Technology and Digital Innovation in 2024. Received significant additional government investment in late 2025.

---

## Mission, Vision, Values

**Mission:** Help planners stay relevant and plan great places and communities using evidence and advanced technology.

**Vision:** Land iQ becomes the default digital tool for planning professionals in NSW, then nationally, then globally. The "Google for planners": ubiquitous, relied upon, and embedded in how planning work gets done.

**Values:**
- Public value first
- Technology to help planners do better work, not replace them
- Fit-for-purpose over feature bloat
- Constant improvement

---

## Product and Value Proposition

**Core capabilities:**
- Multi-criteria site search across the entire NSW cadastre (8M+ lots)
- Scenario planning and bulk rezoning modelling with 3D visualisation
- Interactive spatial dashboards and reports
- Data library consolidating 200+ government data sources
- Property and portfolio analysis
- Collaboration tools with role-based access controls

**USP:** Government-built, government-trusted. Born from a real operational need, not a startup pitch. All-in-one system spanning urban planning, property, and architecture. Already adopted by 128 NSW councils and multiple state agencies.

**Core benefit statement:** A single $8,000 pa licence replaces tens of thousands in consultant reports and manual data collection throughout the year.

**Not yet:** Feasibility studies, full DA assessment automation, AI-native workflows. Labs features (AI integration) are in prototype stage under James Strutt's lead.

## Target Customers

**Segment 1: NSW State Government Agencies**
- Access via government rollout (no sales cycle)
- Use cases: housing delivery, property audits, strategic planning, site identification
- Decision makers: Directors and Executive Directors within DPHI and adjacent agencies

**Segment 2: NSW Local Councils (128 onboarded)**
- Provided access through government program
- Use cases: LEP/DCP reviews, site assessment, development assessment context, strategic land use planning
- Challenge: low activation rate. Training and change management are the bottleneck, not price
- Key contact: strategic planner or GIS officer

**Segment 3: Private Sector, Large Consultancies and Developers**
- Paid subscription at ~$8,000 pa per licence
- Use cases: site identification, due diligence, scenario testing, client presentations
- Longer sales cycle; need to prove ROI against Landchecker, Nearmap, manual processes
- This is where commercial revenue growth has to come from

**Segment 4: LALCs (Local Aboriginal Land Councils)**
- Rollout in progress; specific use cases around land management and community planning
- Requires tailored onboarding

**Segment 5: Researchers and Academics**
- Lower commercial priority but valuable for credibility and case studies

## Competitor Landscape

**Direct:**
- **Landchecker:** Private sector focused, strong UX, won Lendlease. Does not have Land iQ's depth in planning analytics or government data authority.
- **Nearmap:** Aerial imagery and location intelligence. Overlaps on spatial capability, not on planning analytics.
- **CoreLogic (RP Data):** Strong in valuation and market data. Weaker in planning-specific workflows.
- **ePlanning Spatial Viewer / NSW Planning Portal:** Government tool, free, basic. No analytics, no scenario planning.

**Indirect:**
- Manual processes (consultants doing site searches across multiple government websites)
- In-house GIS teams at large councils and consultancies
- Generic GIS platforms (ArcGIS, QGIS) with custom configurations

**Where Land iQ wins:** Depth of government data integration, scenario planning, all-in-one platform, government endorsement, 3D capability.

**Where Land iQ loses:** UX simplicity (Landchecker is slicker for quick searches), brand awareness in private sector, speed of feature development (WSP constraints).
